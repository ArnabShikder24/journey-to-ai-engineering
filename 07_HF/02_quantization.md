Quantization:
Quantization is a technique used to reduce the size of machine learning models and improve their inference speed by converting the model's weights from floating-point precision to lower precision formats, such as int8 or int16. This can lead to faster computations and lower memory usage, making it suitable for deployment on resource-constrained devices.
